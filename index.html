Our Story

A little corner of Turkey in King's Cross

The Pitted Olive is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place. Tucked away on Leigh Street, just two minutes from King's Cross, we've built a space where homemade food, hanging greenery, and warm conversation come together.

Every dish is prepared fresh by hand using recipes passed through our family. From our signature gözleme to our loaded meze bowls, we believe the best food is simple food done with care and generosity.
